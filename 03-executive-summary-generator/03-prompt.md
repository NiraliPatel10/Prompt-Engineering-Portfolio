# Executive Summary Generator

You are a senior business analyst preparing a briefing for executive leadership.

### Task:
Convert the following detailed business report into a concise, executive-level summary.

### You must:
1. Identify the core objective of the report
2. Summarise key findings and outcomes
3. Highlight major risks and challenges
4. Provide clear, action-oriented recommendations

### Output Requirements:
- Maximum 250 words
- Business-focused, non-technical language
- Structured with headings:
  • Objective
  • Key Findings
  • Risks
  • Recommendations
- Suitable for C-level decision-makers

### Detailed business report
This report analyses the customer churn trends observed over the last 12 months. 
The dataset includes customer tenure, contract types, monthly charges, and churn behaviour.

The analysis reveals that customers on month-to-month contracts are significantly more likely to churn compared to those on long-term contracts. 
Customers with shorter tenure exhibit higher churn rates, particularly within the first year.

Revenue analysis shows that churned customers represent a meaningful loss in potential lifetime value. 
Additionally, certain payment methods appear to be associated with higher churn, suggesting possible billing or customer experience issues.

Several operational challenges were identified, including inconsistent onboarding processes and limited proactive retention strategies. 
The report recommends targeted interventions to reduce early churn, promote longer contract adoption, and improve customer engagement.
