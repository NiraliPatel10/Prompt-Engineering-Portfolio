##1. Business Requirements
	-	The organisation requires an online shopping application to enable digital product sales and improve customer accessibility.
	-	The application must support secure customer account creation and management to personalise the shopping experience.
	-	Customers must be able to browse products efficiently by category to enhance product discoverability.
	-	The solution must allow customers to select products and manage them within a shopping cart prior to purchase.
	-	The application must support a seamless checkout process with multiple payment methods to maximise conversion rates.
	-	The system must provide automated email notifications to confirm order placement and status updates.

##2. Functional Requirements
	-	The system shall allow users to register, log in, and manage personal accounts.
	-	The system shall display products organised by predefined categories.
	-	The system shall enable users to add, update, and remove products from a shopping cart.
	-	The system shall calculate order totals, including taxes and delivery charges, during checkout.
	-	The system shall integrate with multiple payment gateways (e.g., credit/debit cards, digital wallets).
	-	The system shall generate and send automated email notifications upon successful order placement.

##3. User Stories
	-	As a customer, I want to create an account so that I can securely manage my orders and personal details.
	-	As a customer, I want to browse products by category so that I can quickly find items of interest.
	-	As a customer, I want to add products to a shopping cart so that I can review my selections before purchasing.
	-	As a customer, I want to choose from multiple payment options during checkout so that I can pay using my preferred method.
	-	As a customer, I want to receive email notifications for my orders so that I am informed about my purchase status.

##4. Non-Functional Requirements
Performance
	•	The system shall support concurrent user access without performance degradation.
	•	Product listing and checkout pages shall load within acceptable response times under normal operating conditions.
Security
	•	The system shall enforce secure authentication and authorisation for all user accounts.
	•	Payment processing shall comply with industry security standards (e.g., PCI-DSS).
	•	User data shall be encrypted in transit and at rest.
Availability & Reliability
	•	The application shall be available 24/7, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.
	•	The system shall ensure data consistency and prevent duplicate order creation.
Usability
	•	The user interface shall be intuitive and accessible across desktop and mobile devices.
	•	The application shall follow accessibility best practices (e.g., WCAG guidelines).
Scalability
	•	The solution shall support future growth in users, products, and transaction volumes without major redesign.

5. Acceptance Criteria
Account Management
	•	Given that a user provides valid details when they register, then an account is successfully created.
	•	Given a registered user, when they log in, they are authenticated and granted access.
Product Browsing
	•	Given that product categories exist, when a user selects a category, then relevant products are displayed.
	•	Given products are listed, when a user selects a product, the product details are shown.
Shopping Cart
	•	Given that a product is selected, when the user adds it to the cart, the cart updates accurately.
	•	Given items are in the cart, when the user modifies quantities or removes items, then totals are recalculated correctly.
Checkout & Payment
	•	Given items are in the cart, when the user proceeds to checkout, then the order summary is displayed.
	•	Given that multiple payment options are available, when the user selects one and completes payment, the order is confirmed.
Notifications
	•	Given that an order is successfully placed, when the transaction is completed, an order confirmation email is sent to the user.

6. High-Level Process Flow
	1	User accesses the online shopping application.
	2	User logs in or registers a new account.
	3	User browses products by category.
	4	User selects products and adds them to the shopping cart.
	5	User reviews the cart and proceeds to checkout.
	6	User selects a payment method and completes payment.
	7	System confirms the order.
	8	System sends an order confirmation email to the user.

7. Assumptions & Dependencies 
Assumptions
	•	Users have access to a valid email address.
	•	Payment gateways are available and operational.
	•	Product catalogue data is maintained by an internal admin system.
Dependencies
	•	Third-party payment gateway integration.
	•	Email service provider for notification delivery.
	•	Hosting infrastructure supporting scalability and availability.
